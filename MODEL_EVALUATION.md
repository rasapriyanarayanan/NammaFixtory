# Model evaluation plan

Current numbers are in `docs/EVALUATION_RESULTS.md` (generated by `python3 scripts/evaluate.py`). They use **synthetic
images and hand-written complaints**, so they show the pipeline works but are **not** real-world accuracy.

## To produce real numbers
1. **Detector.** Convert RDD2020 to YOLO format, add 300+ consented photographs from Tamil Nadu roads and label them. Hold out a local validation set that is never used in training. Run `scripts/train_yolo.py`. Report precision, recall, F1 and mAP50 per class on the **local** set.
2. **Text.** Collect 200+ real or realistic Tamil/English complaints, label them, and compare the keyword lexicon with a fine-tuned multilingual Transformer (for example an Indic BERT). Report per-class F1.
3. **Duplicates.** Label pairs of real reports (same issue / different issue). Report false-duplicate and missed-duplicate rates and tune `THRESHOLD`, `RADIUS_M` and the weights in `ai/duplicates.py`. Consider Sentence-Transformers and image embeddings.
4. **Evidence.** Gather real before/after pairs, including bad evidence (wrong place, unrelated photo). Report how often the system correctly refuses to call evidence consistent.
5. **Recurrence.** Compare flagged locations with engineers' judgement in a pilot ward.
6. **Usability.** Test Tamil and English submission with real users, including elderly users and users on low bandwidth.

Do not claim reductions in potholes, repair time or cost until a real pilot shows them.
